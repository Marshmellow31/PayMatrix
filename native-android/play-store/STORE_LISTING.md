# paymatrix Google Play listing

## App name

paymatrix

## Short description

Split shared expenses, see balances, and record user-confirmed settlements.

## Full description

paymatrix gives friends, families, flatmates, and travel groups one clear place to record shared spending.

Create a group, add an expense, choose who participated, and see what each person owes. Flexible equal, exact, and itemized splits keep the ledger accurate down to the paise. Suggested settlements reduce unnecessary transfers, while an immutable activity history shows who changed a financial record and when.

Use spending logs for personal or shared timelines, add your share from an existing group expense, or scan a receipt and review the extracted details before saving. Previously loaded data remains available offline, and supported expense and spending-log changes wait securely for synchronization.

paymatrix can open an installed UPI app when you choose to pay. Opening a payment app does not prove money moved. A settlement is recorded only after you confirm it, and you should always verify the completed transfer in your bank or UPI history.

Highlights:

- Shared expense groups and clear per-person balances
- Equal, exact, and itemized splitting
- User-confirmed settlement records
- Immutable expense, settlement, and spending-log activity
- Camera or gallery receipt scanning with review before save
- Offline cached viewing and pending synchronization for supported edits
- Native Google sign-in, notifications, data export, and account deletion
- No advertisements and no sale of personal data
