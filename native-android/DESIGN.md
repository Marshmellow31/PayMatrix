# Android visual contract

Operate mode, native Android semantics. Neutral layered charcoal in dark; warm ivory with dark ink in light, based on the supplied Rodeo screenshot. Use semantic Material roles throughout, persistent System/Light/Dark, system-bar appearance matched to the selected theme. No blue glass navbar. Standard Android sans typography; body 14–16sp, readable captions, 48dp targets. Preserve screen hierarchy and financial behavior. Color never alone conveys debt or confirmation. Black-on-white QR is intentionally invariant. Tab transitions are short fades and saved state; disabled system motion is respected. Test both themes, keyboard and 200% text. No marketing claims of superiority without usability evidence.

2.3.1 refinement: 28sp page titles, 16dp shared page inset and section spacing, 14sp body / 12sp metadata. Light actions use sage primaryContainer with dark-green content. Friends/member/activity lists use compact rows; details expand or open a sheet. Widgets show saved snapshots with freshness, native configuration, and matching previews.

Navigation refinement: 22dp outer corners, 6dp container inset, 16dp selected-item corners, 21dp icons and a 3dp icon/label gap. Items retain a 52dp minimum touch height; labels use 11sp/14sp and grow with system text settings.
