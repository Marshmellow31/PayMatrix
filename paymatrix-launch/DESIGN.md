# Shared Moments

Approved campaign direction: calm consumer product film, 28 seconds, 1080 × 2400 at
60fps. Remotion owns every visual and audio beat. No Blender assets are consumed.

Graphite #111a16, environmental green, silver-green #e5ebe5 contrast scenes, restrained
warm reflected color. Existing logo and product dark/white semantics remain intact.
Locally bundled Manrope headlines and Inter UI. Short sentence-case copy with readable
holds. Code-built layered phone shell with live deterministic React screens.

Signature moment: a paper bill becomes an editable review screen, followed by four
individually revealed shares. No perpetual pulsing or automatic payment-success state.

The product components are presentation adapters derived from production structures;
they are not recordings or live authenticated application routes. Preserve this boundary.
The old 18-second composition and its assets remain available as a legacy export.
