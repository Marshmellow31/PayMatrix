# PayMatrix Native Android 2.1.1

## Release Assets
- APK: `paymatrix-native-2.1.1.apk`
- Checksum: `paymatrix-native-2.1.1.apk.sha256`

## Highlights
- **Floating Bill Scanner**: Added floating scan bill FAB in Group Detail view.
- **Group Detail Spacing**: Streamlined GroupHero with 2-button layout ("Add expense" & "Settle up") without text wrapping, and compacted expense rows.
- **Friends & Requests**: Fixed horizontal TabRow to prevent vertical text wrapping; added friend 3-dots modal showing shared groups and settlement status.
- **Navbar & Navigation**: Polished bottom navigation bar pill indicators and spacing.
- **Back Gesture**: Added BackHandler on all modals and dialogs.
- **Spending Logs & Profile**: Added new user onboarding explainer and dedicated UPI payment card.
